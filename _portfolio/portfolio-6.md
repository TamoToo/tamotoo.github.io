### 🎖️ Project & Competition Overview
In this competitive AI tournament, agents competed in real-time matches playing **Divercité**, a complex combinatorial board game featuring high branching factors, spatial resource management, and adversarial multi-step tactics.

---

### Technical Approach

1. **Adversarial Search & Pruning:**
   - Implemented an optimized **Min-Max algorithm with Alpha-Beta pruning** and iterative deepening.
   - Built custom transposition tables with Zobrist hashing to eliminate redundant subtree evaluation.

2. **Policy-Guided Reinforcement Learning Heuristics:**
   - Designed a domain-specific evaluation heuristic trained via self-play Reinforcement Learning to score non-terminal board states accurately.
   - Balanced aggressive territorial control with defensive counter-plays.

---

### Key Takeways & Results
- Secured a **Top 10 ranking** in the global competition against hundreds of competing agents.
- Demonstrates deep mastery of game theory, tree search optimization, state representation, and heuristic formulation.
