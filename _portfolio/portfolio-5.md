### 🧪 Research Internship — CEA-List (Grenoble, France)
*Aug 2023 – Feb 2024* | **Role:** Cognitive AI & Reinforcement Learning Researcher

---

### Project Overview
At CEA-List, this research investigated how neuroscience-inspired cognitive architectures can be bridged with modern Reinforcement Learning frameworks to simulate, predict, and align with complex human behavioral decision-making under uncertainty.

---

### Technical Highlights

1. **SOAR Cognitive Modeling:**
   - Formalized human procedural, declarative, and working memory state transitions within the **SOAR cognitive architecture**.
   - Modeled goal-directed reasoning, rule-based conflict resolution, and sub-goal chunking.

2. **Custom Gymnasium Simulation Pipeline:**
   - Engineered a custom **OpenAI Gymnasium environment** completely from scratch.
   - Built vectorized observation spaces, dynamic physics state transitions, and custom reward shaping functions.
   - Trained and benchmarked discrete and continuous RL policy gradient agents.

---

### Key Takeaways
- Deep practical expertise in environment construction, reward engineering, and cognitive architecture integration.
- Demonstrates strong foundational proficiency in both theoretical cognitive science and practical PyTorch/Gymnasium RL pipelines.
